Making perfect homemade lasagna doesn’t have to be tedious. This top-rated easy lasagna recipe comes together quickly with a relatively short ingredient list.

Easy Lasagna Ingredients

    This easy lasagna starts with:
  • Beef: You can use ground turkey for a lighter option.
  • Spaghetti sauce: Use store-bought or create a homemade one
  • Lasagna noodles:You can use the stored ones
  • Onion:It gives spicy flavor
  • Mozzarella cheese:Everybody love cheese (even the lactose intolerants)
  • Tomato:It give great flavor to the beef
  • Oven:To cook it
  • Baking dish:To put the pasta there and cook it
  • Aluminum foil:To wrap it up, and get a better coction
  • Spatula:It will help to take off the lasagna form the oven and made it slices
