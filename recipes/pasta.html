Pasta Recipe

Pasta is always good for special moments with your spcial people !

Making perfect homemade pasta doesn’t have to be tedious. This top-rated easy lasagna recipe comes together quickly with a relatively short ingredient list.

Ingredients

    This recipe starts with:
  • Spaghetti: Use any type of pasta you like.
  • Eggs: They make the carbonara creamy.
  • Parmesan cheese: It gives the pasta a delicious cheesy flavor.
  • Bacon: You can use pancetta for a more traditional option.
  • Black pepper: It adds a spicy and delicious flavor.
  • Salt: To season the pasta water.
  • Water: To cook the pasta.
  • Pot: To boil the pasta.
  • Pan: To cook the bacon.
  • Fork: To mix the eggs and cheese.
